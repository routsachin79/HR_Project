# HR_Project
To share my latest HR Analytics Dashboard, developed using Microsoft Power BI to analyze employee workforce trends, attrition patterns, demographics, and job satisfaction.  The goal of this project was to transform raw HR data into an interactive dashboard that can support data-driven HR decision-making.

📊 Key HR KPIs

🔹 Total Employees
🔹 Active Employees
🔹 Total Attrition
🔹 Attrition Rate
🔹 Average Employee Age
🔹 Average Monthly Salary

📈 Key Insights & Visualizations

📌 Employees by Age Group
📌 Attrition by Age
📌 Attrition by Department
📌 Attrition by Job Role
📌 Job Satisfaction Rating
📌 Attrition by Education Field

🎛️ Interactive Filters

The dashboard allows HR professionals to drill down into workforce data using:
• Gender
• Marital Status
• Education/Degree
• Business Travel — Non-Travel, Travel Frequently & Travel Rarely

💡 Business Value

This dashboard can help HR teams identify:

✅ High-attrition departments and job roles
✅ Employee demographic patterns
✅ Workforce composition
✅ Potential retention challenges
✅ Job satisfaction trends
✅ Salary and workforce patterns
